About this diary

Why this page exists

This diary is a place for the personal side of the work: the family lessons, classroom notes, faith questions, practical reflections, and ordinary moments that do not belong in a résumé or an institutional update.

Some entries may be polished essays. Others may be short field notes. The point is not performance. The point is recordkeeping, reflection, and telling the truth carefully.
